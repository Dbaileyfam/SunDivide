About the band

Sun Divide is a Salt Lake City–based band formed in 2015 by singer/songwriter Thom Welsh, blending Reggae, Rock, Celtic, and African music into a distinctive, genre-defying sound.

Following the critically acclaimed 2017 EP Lately, Sun Divide gained momentum as an opening act for The Wailers. The catalog has grown with singles Everything's Right and Life, plus the Closer to Home EP.

The group has performed at Reggae Rise Up and continues to build a reputation for positive, groove-forward live sets suited to clubs, festivals, and concert stages.

Press

“Life by Sun Divide is a song that works as a sound embrace for those who go through moments of doubt… falling is not failing, getting lost is not definitive. The repetition of phrases like ‘life happens one breath at a time’ reinforces the idea that change does not happen suddenly, but in small acts of daily resistance.”

“Musically, the song mixes a soft reggae base with elements of alternative rock and nuances of world music… Thom Welsh sounds close, almost conversational, as if the song were a piece of advice between friends rather than a grandiloquent hymn.”
