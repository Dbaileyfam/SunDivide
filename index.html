About the band

Sun Divide is a Salt Lake City–based band formed in 2015 by singer/songwriter Thom Welsh, blending Reggae, Rock, Celtic, and African music into a distinctive, genre-defying sound.

Following the critically acclaimed 2017 EP Lately, Sun Divide gained momentum as an opening act for The Wailers. The catalog has grown with singles Everything's Right and Life, plus the Closer to Home EP.

The group has performed at Reggae Rise Up and continues to build a reputation for positive, groove-forward live sets suited to clubs, festivals, and concert stages.
